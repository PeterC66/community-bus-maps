Everyone currently signed in. Sessions last 7 days and slide forward on use, so an account that stops being used loses its credential within a week. Revoking one signs that browser out immediately — the way to deal with a lost laptop or a token that has been somewhere it should not.

A session is named by a short handle, never by its token: a list of live tokens would be a list of accounts anyone reading this screen could become. Publishing, changing an organisation's settings and changing a user's role each need a sign-in from the last 30 minutes, however long the session itself has left.
