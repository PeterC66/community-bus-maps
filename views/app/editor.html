Save & download

Save is only for your own edits — colours, landmarks, operators. Making them above and saving renders a new numbered version and its print-ready files; nothing is deleted, and every earlier version stays listed below. You never have to download anything: the files are here whenever you want them.

Getting it published

A version stays a private draft until you send it to us and an approver reviews it. Only an approver can publish — publishing sets the official, print-ready version people rely on.

Public page

Once a version is published, we give it a page on our public site that anyone can view, print or download. Your organisation’s name, badge and colour come from your public details.

“Changes coming” banner

Shown above the map image on the public page, to warn readers of a known upcoming change this map doesn't reflect yet. We suggest wording automatically from upcoming service changes; edit it if it reads oddly, or clear it. It clears itself once this map is next published.
