title: "The operations handbook did not mention the fourth role at all"
---

`H1-operations-handbook.md` §3 lists who does what and where they do it, and the local adviser went live without appearing in it. The vetting policy had the judgement and `DEVELOPING.md` had the code, but the one page an operator reads to remember what the jobs *are* said nothing.

It now carries the two rows — asking somebody, on the admin **Advisers** tab, and the adviser's own page — and a paragraph making the point that matters operationally: **this is not a fourth hat.** The other three roles are all Peter. A local adviser is somebody outside the operation entirely, who reaches one map's current version on screen with no download and nowhere to write anything down, because the answering half is not built and replies still arrive as email.

Plus the two gotchas that cost time on the day the seat shipped: a **switched-off account cannot be asked**, because no sign-in link can be issued for one — the console refuses and names the remedy — and the console now reports whether the invitation was **actually emailed**, so *"no sign-in link was emailed"* should be read as the outcome rather than a footnote.

Documentation only; nothing served to anybody changes.
