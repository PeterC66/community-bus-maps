date: 2026-09-01
title: "The review screen could not see a landmark answer, and a document promised it saw everything"
---

- **A publish review reported *nothing changed* about a version that had changed three things.** The landmark chooser shipped this morning writing `overrides.internal.poiTiers`, and `changeSummary()` diffed `routeColors` and `internal.pois` only. So a version that dropped one landmark, promoted another to *always show* and renamed a third came back `unchanged: true` — the review screen told an approver there was nothing to look at, and the editor advised the customer to "make an edit and save first". Found by re-reading the documentation rather than by any check.
- **The document is the sharp end of it.** *README - How to publish a map to the portal.md* has said, in bold, that what the review screen shows is **not a summary of the changes but all of them — nothing else can have moved**. That is the sentence an approver leans on to publish quickly, and for a few hours it was false. It has been corrected, and the near-miss written into it, because the general lesson outlives this instance: **a completeness claim is a claim about a JOIN between what a customer can write and what a reviewer is shown, and adding a new thing a customer can write breaks it silently.** Nothing goes red; the screen simply says less.
- **`changeSummary()` now reports tiers and renames separately**, and in the reviewer's words rather than the engine's — *always show* / *show if there is room* / *do not show*, never `must`/`may`/`miss`. Separately, because they say different things: a tier decides whether a place is on the sheet at all, a rename changes what the sheet calls it, and folding them together is exactly how a rename would have stayed invisible. `overridesUnchanged` counts both, so the publish gate can no longer call such a version identical to the published one.
- **`scripts/test-change-summary.mjs` holds the case in fourteen assertions**, including the two that would otherwise rot: a rename with no tier move is still a change, and a version compared with **itself** is still unchanged — without that control, "not unchanged" could be satisfied by a diff that always reports something.
- **The customer guide was two states out of date.** *C1* said landmarks were "switch icons on or off". It now describes the chooser, and says plainly what the older tick box does differently: unticking stops the icon being **drawn** and keeps its space reserved, while *Do not show* takes the place off the map and actually gives the room back — which is why a sheet re-arranges when you save one.
