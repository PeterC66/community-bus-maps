- **Five authorities no longer read as publishing a map of the whole network** (buses-data OA-381). Cambridgeshire and Peterborough, South Yorkshire, North Lincolnshire, Liverpool City Region and Transport for London were graded `networkMap.status: yes` although each row's own note said the map covered less than the network — Cambridgeshire's six district PDFs show the precept-funded routes only. Peter found it by searching for Boxworth. The panel said *Publishes a map of the whole network*, and the letter a reader sends their council said *I can see that you publish a map of the whole network*. buses-data now grades those five rows `some`, a value Peter approved on 2026-09-25, and the vendored `public/data/bus-map-directory.json` is re-synced from it.
- **`offerOf()` offers a `some` row exactly as it offers a whole-network one**, with status `network`, and sets `networkPart`. The card and the letter's two sentences say *a map of part of the network* when it is set. Without the code change the five rows would have fallen into the else branch and read *publishes no bus map that we could find*. `test-directory` showed that on the re-synced file before the fix: Cambridgeshire read `none`.
- **Tested and seen to fail.** `test-suggest` gains a partial-network letter and card that must say *part of the network* and never *whole network*, and a whole-network control that must still say *whole network*. `test-directory` accepts `some` in the `networkMap` vocabulary and requires every `some` row to be offered as a partial network map. Both new checks went red against the unchanged code, and both are green with it.
- **Deploy history, written before the deploy per [DEPLOY §3b](docs/DEPLOY.md#3b-the-deploy-history-entry-is-written-before-the-deploy-and-merged-with-it).** This pull request's merge is the commit that goes live. It changes three sentences of public text, one field of the directory offer and five rows of vendored data. There is no schema, route, engine or rendered-sheet change, so `verify:area` and `verify:place` do not move.
