title: "A map is not delivered while one of its blocking local questions is open"
---

- **`deliver-map.mjs` has a step 0c: no blocking local decision outstanding** (buses-data OA-083). Every map build records what only somebody local can tell us in the map folder's `local-decisions.json`, and marks `blocking` the questions where our printed guess could misdirect a passenger. Until now nothing between the build and the live site read that mark. Peter ruled on 2026-09-26 that those questions go to the customer by letter, so the delivery now refuses, before the `scp`, while any blocking decision's answer is anything but `answered` or `dont-know`. That includes a state the gate has not learned. A map with no `local-decisions.json` passes as N/A, and a file it cannot read refuses as CANNOT TELL.
- **Day one is green by deferral, not by exemption.** Three maps held an open blocking question when the gate landed: Ramsey, St Neots Town Centre and The Shelfords. Each has a dated entry in the new `scripts/local-decision-waivers.json` until 2026-10-31. The entries are per DECISION and never per map, so a new blocking question from a rebuild is refused even on a map that has a waiver. `--local-decisions-unchecked "<reason>"` is the one-off escape hatch, as with steps 0 and 0b.
- **`test:local-decisions` and `test:prove-red-local-decisions`** cover every answer state, every way of not knowing, the waiver matching and expiry, the shipped waiver file, and the gate's wiring before the `scp`. The prove-red harness has six arms, and each goes red for its own reason.
- **Deploy history, written before the deploy per [DEPLOY §3b](docs/DEPLOY.md#3b-the-deploy-history-entry-is-written-before-the-deploy-and-merged-with-it).** This pull request's merge is the commit that goes live. It changes a laptop-side delivery script, its library, tests and docs only. The running server's code, schema and routes, the engine and the rendered sheets are unchanged, so `verify:area` and `verify:place` do not move.
