title: "The vendored engine gains three shared modules, and the check that should have noticed the third was blind to it"
---

Nine engine files re-vendored and **three new ones added** — `engine_paths.js`, `page.js` and `external_primitives.js`. The skill-side change is [OA-224](https://github.com/PeterC66/buses-data) Tier 3.4 and 3.5 in `buses-data`, claude-skills `5816627`: four spellings of one dependency resolver became one, the page size stopped being twelve numbers that happened to be equal, `esc` stopped being nine identical functions, and the marks the external sheets are drawn out of stopped being two copies of one file. Nothing drawn moved — all 98 sheet verdicts on the skill side are identical to the baseline, and here `verify:area`, `verify:place`, `verify:defaults` and `test:selfsufficient` all PASS byte-for-byte against the committed fixtures.

- **`requireScan()` said the engine was complete when it was missing a module, and this repository's own comment had predicted it.** The scan enumerates what the vendored CODE asks for, precisely so that a generator arriving with a new dependency cannot be silently half-vendored. It knew three idioms; `external_primitives.js` is required by all three external generators through a FOURTH — `path.join(path.dirname(_LABELLER), 'x.js')` and its factored spelling `_from('x.js')` — which pins a module to the folder another was already found in rather than searching for it. The header of `scripts/lib/vendored.mjs` had named that arithmetic as something the scan could not see, and added that it "would not be caught if a future edit introduced them for the first time". A future edit did. Left alone, the vendored radial would have thrown `MODULE_NOT_FOUND` on the first external render on the VPS, where there is no skill tree to fall back to.
- **So pattern 4 is now in the scan, and `test-vendored.mjs` watches it fail.** Two new cases build a throwaway engine using each spelling and require the audit to report `UNRESOLVED`; both were confirmed red with the pattern removed and green with it back. The `&&` candidate-list form stays excluded for the reason it always was — it is one of three candidates `existsSync()` chooses between, and flagging it would open this check red on `diagram_internal.js` and `schematize_internal.js`. `qr.js`, required lazily inside `footer.js`, is now the whole of what this scan still cannot see, and the header says so.
- **`test:selfsufficient` is the independent confirmation.** It reports 21 modules loaded from `engine/` and one from the map's own data pack, and `engine_paths.js` and `page.js` are named in that list — so the internal sheet really is being drawn by the vendored tree and not by anything left on the machine.
- **`vendor-engine.mjs --add` did the three rows**, which is the half of the hand-off added in [#198](https://github.com/PeterC66/community-bus-maps/pull/198) landing its first real use: the SHA-256 is written from the bytes that arrived rather than typed into a JSON file by hand.
- **What did NOT come across, on purpose.** `gen_external_radial.js` and `gen_external_places.js` wrap a one-word label longer than their wrap width onto two lines, the first of them empty — a live defect on three published sheets, all of them the destination "Hinchingbrooke". The correct implementation is in `external_primitives.js` as `wrap`; both generators name the legacy one explicitly so nobody inherits it. Fixing it moves ink and needs a version bump and a re-render per sheet, so it is `buses-data` OA-229 and a separate delivery.
