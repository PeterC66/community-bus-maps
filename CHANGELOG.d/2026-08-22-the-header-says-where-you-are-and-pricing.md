---
date: 2026-08-22
title: "The header says where you are, and Pricing says what accessibility you get"
---

- **The public nav now marks the current page.** Every navlink looked identical on every page, so the header told a visitor nothing about where they were. `public/js/nav-current.js` sets `aria-current="page"` on the matching link and `styles.css` hangs a rule under it. **Done in JS deliberately**: the header is one string in `scripts/lib/site-chrome.mjs` copied verbatim into all 14 pages and `check-chrome.mjs` asserts they still match byte for byte, so a per-page attribute would trade that guarantee for a cosmetic gain — and the current page is something the browser already knows.
- **Two signals, and no layout shift.** The link goes to full text colour *and* gains an underline, so it does not depend on colour alone — the same rule the maps follow. The underline is an absolutely positioned `::after`, not a border, padding or font-weight change, because any of those would alter the link's box and nudge the rest of the header sideways as you moved between pages. Verified in both colour schemes against the real computed styles; the rule is `--primary` on both, `#1b4db3` on white and `#5a8dff` on near-black.
- **A map page counts as "Published maps".** `/m/<slug>` and `/m/<slug>/services` highlight *Published maps*, because the visitor reached them from that section and has not left it. The home page highlights nothing — the logo is already the home indicator. Pages absent from the nav (accessibility, legal, terms, opportunity) highlight nothing, which is correct rather than a gap.
- **Pricing now says what accessibility is included.** *"All the standard outputs for your map"* was the only thing the Included list said about the largest single reason a council or school can buy this at all. It now names the screen-reader-readable service list, route numbers as well as colours, and the WCAG 2.2 AA aim, and links to `/accessibility.html` for the detail — including what is *not* fixed. Kept to two lines on purpose: a first draft ran to eight and unbalanced the Included card against Costs Extra; both cards are now the same height again.
