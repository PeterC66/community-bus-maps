title: "The landmark control that had never run where CI looks"
---

- **The landmark-tier suite's control asserted something false, and vendoring the de-duplication engine turned it red on the laptop.** It read *with nothing classified, the selector and the drawn sheet agree exactly*, and that equality is not a property of this engine: March offers 23 candidates and draws 22 symbols, because its unnamed sports centre arrives as `leisure:Leisure` and OA-338 made a category label stop counting as a name. It passed for as long as it did because the one town it was ever measured against is the first in map-id order — St Ives shows the same shape in `allotments:Allotments`, and High Wycombe loses four more `may` candidates to its `coreBox` frame long after selection.

- **What replaces it is the direction the rest of the block actually leans on.** The sheet can draw nothing the selector did not offer, and nothing the selector missed can reach the sheet. Candidates selected and then culled by the frame are printed with the run rather than asserted, because nothing in the selector's report can see a frame — `culledAfterTiers` is buses-data OA-250 item 2 and is not built.

- **The half of the claim that needs no generator now runs where CI looks, and that is the larger half of this change.** The whole block needed a real map pack under the ignored data directory, so seven assertions and the single prove-red arm standing under them had never run in CI once since 2026-09-01 — measured by moving the map packs aside, at which point the suite prints its skip line and both scripts exit 0. It now writes its own four-node OpenStreetMap fixture and a routes.json into a temp dir, so the key universe, the miss-carried-in-the-pack case and the new exactness control are falsifiable anywhere. The pack half stays, because only a real pack exercises the render side.

- **The exactness the old control was reaching for is kept, and stated where it can be true.** On the fixture, candidates minus the misses is precisely what the selector itself returns — which is what stops every superset assertion below it being satisfied by a selector that simply returns more.

- **Five new prove-red arms, three of them needing no pack.** 27 mutations each caught by the assertion named for them on this laptop, and 24 with the map packs moved aside, the two render-side arms reporting themselves skipped rather than passing quietly. 86 tests, 0 failed, in both shapes.
