---
date: 2026-10-02
title: "The site now wears the BusMaps.uk logo, colours and typeface"
---

- **The header shows the BusMaps.uk logo instead of the bus emoji, on every page**, including the signed-in app and the not-found page. In dark mode it switches to the white version of the same logo, so it stays readable (buses-data OA-527).
- **The browser tab shows the new icon.** The old icon was the emoji drawn by your device, so it looked different on every phone and computer. It is now a green tile with a bus route and a marigold stop, and phones that save the site to the home screen use the same picture.
- **Green and marigold replace the blue and amber**, in both light and dark mode, and the page background is now a warm paper colour. Marigold is the one accent: it marks both the brand and the "read this" notices.
- **The text is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible**, a typeface designed by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision, and the site now carries its own copy rather than using whatever your device has. The 0 and the letter O look different in it.
- **A customer's own map colour is unchanged.** The colour an organisation picks for its map is still the one it picked.
