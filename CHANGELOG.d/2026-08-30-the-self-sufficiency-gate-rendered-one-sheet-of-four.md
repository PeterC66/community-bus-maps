date: 2026-08-30
title: "The self-sufficiency gate rendered one sheet of the four it certified"
---

- **`test-engine-selfsufficient.mjs` printed `✓ the vendored engine is self-sufficient` on the strength of the INTERNAL sheet alone (OA-051).** It built a scratch pack of `*.json`, copied `engine/place/gen_internal.js` into it and rendered. Measured on 2026-08-30: 17 modules from `engine/`, one from the pack, verdict green. Nothing in it said anything about the external, the schematic or the diagram — and the external is the one that moved. `gen_external_radial.js` began requiring `dash_fit.js` at load that same day; a delivered pack carries no sibling modules, so that require has to resolve through `SKILL_ASSETS` into `engine/`. Had it not, every area map delivered after that date would have failed to render its external sheet at preview time, and the gate whose whole job is that question would still have printed its tick.
- **It now runs every generator the fixture's `routes.json` declares**, the way `verify-reproduce.mjs` already picks its targets, and asserts per sheet: it ran, it wrote, and every module it pulled came from `engine/` or the pack. Four sheets on the St Ives area fixture — 17 modules on the internal, 7 on the external (`dash_fit.js` among them), 19 on each expert style.
- **The obvious version of this row was a trap and was not built.** An "area fixture with no generators in it" would be a permanently green fixture: `verify-reproduce.mjs` pairs a generator with a shipped SVG, finds none, prints *"nothing to check"* and exits 0. The delivery path already had a gate; it just did not cover enough.
- **It reproduces `generateSvg()`'s TWO invocations rather than picking one.** A map's own generators run out of the data pack; the portal-owned P7 expert styles run from an absolute path in `engine/expert/`. Those are not interchangeable — `gen_internal_schematic.js` finds its pre-stage with `__dirname`, so copying it into the pack breaks it, and the pre-stage then spawns the map's own `gen_internal.js` out of the pack, so a json-only pack breaks it the other way. A gate that renders its subject differently from the way production does is a gate on a procedure nobody runs.
- **The module recorder had to become a `--require` preload, and finding that out was the point of trying.** Hooking `Module._load` in the test's own process saw nothing at all of the schematic or the diagram: both spawn children, and the real loading happens two processes down. `NODE_OPTIONS` carries the recorder into every child, and the per-sheet *"it loaded modules out of `engine/` at all"* check is what says it arrived. **Forward slashes in that variable, measured rather than guessed:** Node parses `NODE_OPTIONS` with shell-like quoting, so a Windows path inside the quotes has its backslashes eaten and every generator dies in the module loader — the first run of the widened gate failed all twelve of its checks that way, wearing the face of a real finding.
- **Falsified, and each arm has to go red by itself** — `npm run test:prove-red-selfsufficient`, wired into `verify.yml` ahead of the gate it falsifies, ~30 s. Three mutations on a scratch copy of `engine/` and `scripts/`, never on the repository: remove `engine/dash_fit.js` and the external arm must redden alone; remove `engine/expert/schematize_internal.js` and the schematic arm must redden alone, which is what proves the P7 arm is really executing two processes down; and hand it a fixture whose `routes.json` no longer declares `internalDiagram` and it must certify **three** sheets, not four. Plus an intact control, because every arm going red at once would mean the copy was broken and not the gate.
- **The count is asserted, not just the verdicts, and that is the lesson rather than the code.** Coverage was this gate's actual bug and a row of ticks cannot express it: one sheet of four printed exactly the same closing line that four of four would have. The closing line now names the number, and the harness reads it back.
- **Found while doing it, and NOT fixed here: this laptop's `.env` still points `FIXTURE_DIR` at `Areas/St Ives/S5-render/v6.55_2026-08-24_1603`,** four versions and six days behind the render the committed fixture was refreshed from on 2026-08-30. So the laptop gate and CI are testing different packs again — the split OA-051 recorded as settled. The gate says so out loud now (the fixture's own `gen_external.js` matches neither vendored candidate byte-for-byte), which is how it was noticed. Raised as its own row.
