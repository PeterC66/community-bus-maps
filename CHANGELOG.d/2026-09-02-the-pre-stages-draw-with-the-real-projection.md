title: "The two expert pre-stages take their projection from the engine, and a fifth shared module arrives"
---

Four engine files re-vendored and **one new one added**, `internal_roads_config.js`. The skill-side change is `buses-data` OA-230 (Tier 4.2 and 4.3 of the 2026-09-01 codebase review), claude-skills `e554511`. Nothing drawn moved — all 98 sheet verdicts on the skill side are identical to the baseline, and here `verify:place`, `verify` and `test:selfsufficient` PASS byte-for-byte against the committed fixtures, with 22 modules now loaded from `engine/` and one from the map's data pack.

- **`expert/diagram_internal.js` and `expert/schematize_internal.js` no longer carry their own projection.** Each held a 40-line copy of the geographic sheet's projection commented "EXACT copy", and the copy had drifted from `projection.js` in four ways — a flat 205 mm frame bottom, no `design.fixedOrientation`, no `overrides.json` rotation, no detail lenses. Both now call `projection.js` and hand it exactly what the copy computed, so the schematic and diagram a map serves today are byte-identical to what they were. Adopting the module's real frame is a drawing change with a version bump per sheet; it was measured (seven of the thirteen sheets would move, six would not) and is with Peter, not in this PR.
- **`internal_roads_config.js` is the one reading of a map's `internalRoads` block.** The geographic generator defaulted nine keys and read an absent key as on; the two pre-stages defaulted three and refused an absent key with exit 1. All three now call one function, and the pre-stages refuse only `internalRoads:false`, which is the generator's own rule. It is required by three vendored generators, so `requireScan()` named it `UNRESOLVED` on the first vendor pass and `vendor-engine --add` wrote its row from the bytes that arrived.
- **`strict_guards.js` now states the contract every generator here is run under**: stdout for progress, stderr for anything the caller must read (written on a zero exit too), and what exit codes 0 to 3 mean — including that an expert pre-stage whose `routes.json` does not opt in exits 0 having written nothing, which `src/expert/index.js` already treats as "not offered" rather than as a failure.
- **What this means for stored maps.** The two pre-stages and `gen_boarding.js` are inside an engine hash for the first time (the town template for the pre-stages, a `boarding/` half of the place template for the boarding plan), so a change to any of them now moves a stamp. `track:engine --apply` is owed on the LIVE store after this deploys, per `docs/DEPLOY.md` §4a; the laptop's count is not the live store's.
