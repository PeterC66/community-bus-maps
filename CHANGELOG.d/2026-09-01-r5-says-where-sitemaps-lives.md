title: "R5 says where the Sitemaps page actually is, and records that Google verification is done"
---

- **The runbook said *open Sitemaps in the left-hand navigation* and that was not enough to find it.** In Search Console *Sitemaps* is not a top-level item — it sits inside the **Indexing** group, below *Pages*, and the operator went looking for it during the real submission on 2026-09-01. R5 now names the group, and carries a direct URL for this property so the navigation does not have to be described at all. A procedure is only as good as its first unfamiliar reader, and this one had never been walked before today.
- **A sentence in the same change was itself written from memory, and was wrong.** It said the *Add a new sitemap* field comes prefixed with `https://busmaps.uk/` so the thing to type is `sitemap.xml`. It does not; it is labelled *Enter sitemap URL* and takes the whole thing. Nothing here could have contradicted it — the console is not something this repository can reach, so a claim about its UI reads exactly like the claims that are checked, which is *an untested claim in the same font* a second time in three days. What caught it was somebody standing in front of the screen. The runbook now says the field is **not** prefixed, in those words, so the next reader is told the thing that was got wrong rather than merely not told the wrong thing.
- **And it now says to expect *Couldn't fetch* on the first look.** That was the status within a minute of the real submission, and it is the state before Google has tried at all rather than a failed fetch — *Type* Unknown, *Last read* blank, *Discovered pages* 0, read across the row. Everything checkable on our side was checked at the time and was fine: `200` as Googlebot, `application/xml`, a valid `urlset` of 48 `<url>` elements with no BOM, nothing in `robots.txt`, no rate limiter on the route, and `200` over **both** IPv4 and IPv6 — that last being the one worth keeping in the runbook, because an `AAAA` record pointing at a host that does not answer is the textbook cause of this message and is invisible to a `curl` on a v4-only connection. The read-back named is URL Inspection → Test live URL, because it is the only instrument in reach that can distinguish *not tried yet* from *tried and failed*.
- **The Google half of `buses-data` OA-015 is done: the domain property for `busmaps.uk` is verified.** Recorded on Peter's word, because that is the only kind of evidence that exists — this is the one step in the whole procedure with no read-back anywhere in either repository, which is exactly why it is written down here rather than left in a console nobody else can see. Bing is next, by *Import from Google Search Console*.
