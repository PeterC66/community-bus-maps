---
date: 2026-08-31
title: "A one-way loop leaves the frame twice, and only one of those tails is a departure"
---

- **Re-vendors `gen_internal.js`: a frame-exit caption may now carry its own preposition, and one that does is printed as written (OA-175, OA-193).** Every caption on every sheet has read `to X` since the device was built, and for a route that runs both ways down the same streets that is the whole truth — the bus goes to X along that tail and comes back from X along it. A **one-way loop** leaves the frame twice, by different roads, and only one of the two is a departure. Ramsey's X31 arrives past Marriotts Drove and leaves past Daintree Road; captioning both *to Peterborough* tells a reader standing at the first one that they can catch a bus to Peterborough there, and they cannot.
- **The reader who found it said so, and could not be answered at the time.** The Ramsey defect that started this arrived through the report-a-problem form; the same message said the captions should read *from* rather than *to*. It was left open on purpose, because the drawn line stopped halfway through the town and an arrow sat where the drawing had ended rather than where the bus goes. Once the loop is drawn as a loop, the claim is checkable and it is right: the drawn path starts at sheet 97.86,34.89 and ends at 44.19,55.08, so the northern cluster is the arrival and the western one the departure.
- **Deliberately a wording rule and not a geometry one.** Which tail of a loop is the arrival is a fact about the timetable, the config author has it, and no amount of looking at the polyline recovers it. `internalRoads.termini[route].start` / `.end` now take `"from Peterborough"` as readily as `"Peterborough"`.
- **Byte-inert on everything already shipped, asserted rather than remembered.** None of the estate's 88 committed terminus labels begins with `to ` or `from `, so all 39 sheets across 20 maps reproduce byte-for-byte — `status.js` after the change, `npm run verify:area`, `verify:place` and `verify:defaults` here. The area fixture is refreshed in `buses-data` in the same round, because a fixture frozen alongside an engine change passes its own gate by construction.
- **What a byte gate could not have told us, and what covers it instead.** The gates prove the *fallback* branch and only by luck — no committed label exercises the new one, so all 39 sheets are identical whether the rule works, is inverted, or was never written. `test/exit_caption.test.js` in `claude-skills` is the cover: it extracts the helper rather than re-implementing it, exercises both branches, checks that only a whole leading word counts (Tonbridge and Frome must not be printed bare), and walks every terminus label in the estate to assert they are all still in the fallback branch — so the first config that adds one says so.
- **The helper's regex shipped broken for four minutes and the test is why it did not ship at all.** The patch that wrote it went through a shell heredoc, which ate the backslash in `\s`; `/^(?:to|from)s/` matched nothing, and `exitCaption('to Huntingdon')` returned `to to Huntingdon`. Every byte gate would have been green.
- **After merge, run `npm run track:engine -- --apply` here and on the VPS.** Tracking is the 2026-08-27 decision, and a re-vendor that stops at `engine/` reaches no map already delivered.
