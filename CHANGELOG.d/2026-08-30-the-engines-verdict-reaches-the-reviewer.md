---
date: 2026-08-30
title: "The engine's verdict on a build now reaches the person reviewing it"
---

- **`build-warnings.txt` travels with a delivery, and the review screen shows it.** The bus skill writes one beside every S4 and S5 run — a count line, then the warnings, where BLOCKING means the engine refused to draw something or drew a label that names nothing. There were **161 of them** on the map tree and the string `build-warnings` appeared **nowhere in this repository**: not in `src/`, not in `scripts/`, not in `engine/`. So the verdict was computed, was correct, was acted on once by whoever happened to be watching the rollout terminal, and was then thrown away. `import-map.mjs` and `propose-update.mjs` now carry the file with the payload, and because accepting an update swaps the staged folder in as the live data, it reaches the map itself by the route the rest of the payload already takes.
- **It is carried rather than re-derived, and that is the whole design.** The portal renders a NEW version with `STRICT_GUARDS=1` and gets its own answer; for the version being *imported* it has none, and re-running the guards would give **today's** engine's opinion of an older pack. The severity contract itself widened on 2026-08-28 — a buried `mapNotes` entry became BLOCKING, and a generator that died stopped being the mildest verdict the module has — so a rebuild classifies things the delivered build never flagged. What an approver needs is the verdict the sheet actually shipped under.
- **An absent report says so, in as many words, and never reports zero.** That is the assertion this change exists to get right: *no warnings* and *we do not know* look identical when the answer is simply missing, and a false zero would tell an approver the engine was happy with a sheet it had refused to draw — strictly worse than silence, because it is the answer they would act on. Every pack delivered before today carries no file, so this is the common case rather than the edge one. An unrecognised file reports the same way instead of guessing.
- **`scripts/test-build-warnings.mjs`** — 24 assertions across the three states, plus the blocking case the corpus cannot supply (the rollout *stops* on a blocking warning, which is why no real example exists on disk). Its last arm parses three real reports off the Buses map tree when that tree is present, and prints **SKIPPED** rather than passing when it is not: a check that could not run must not report a pass it never made.
