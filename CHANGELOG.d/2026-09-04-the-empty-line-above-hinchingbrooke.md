title: "The empty line above Hinchingbrooke, and a chemist nobody named stops taking up room"

Five engine files re-vendored. This is the delivery [the three-modules entry](2026-09-02-the-engine-arrives-with-three-new-modules.md) promised two days ago under *What did NOT come across, on purpose*: the wrap defect that was named, left alone and handed to `buses-data` OA-229 so that it could move ink under its own version bump rather than inside a refactor claiming nothing had moved. OA-229, OA-234 and OA-238 are retired; the skill-side change is claude-skills [`74c8c19`](https://github.com/PeterC66/claude-skills/commit/74c8c19) and the estate rollout is buses-data [`6c62c42`](https://github.com/PeterC66/buses-data/commit/6c62c42).

- **`external_primitives.js` has one wrap now, and it is the right one.** It carried two: `wrap`, correct and called by nothing since `gen_external_busway.js` was dropped, and `wrapLegacyEmptyFirstLine`, which both external generators took and which pushed a one-word label longer than the wrap width onto line two, emitting a real empty `<text></text>` element in front of it. The `firstWordTakesLineOne` flag goes with the caller that wanted it `false`. **On St Ives' external the empty element disappears and *Hinchingbrooke* rises 2 mm to sit centred in a box it had been sitting a full line low inside**; on both Godmanchester place externals *Hinchingbrooke Hospital* — 23 characters — stops being drawn entirely on line two of a box whose width formula expected at most 13, and wraps as `Hinchingbrooke` + `Hospital`. Three published sheets, which is exactly what the row predicted, and no fourth.
- **A point of interest with no name is no longer drawn by default.** `classify()` returns an empty name only for a pharmacy or a GP surgery — every other category has a fallback — and such a symbol costs the same 4.2 × 4.2 mm box and the same placer anchor as a named one, for a bare glyph nobody chose. It now defaults to `miss`. **It is still offered in the landmark chooser**, carrying that tier, so a local can name it with a rename (which promotes it) or agree with the default; a place absent from that list could not be shown as missed and could never be turned back on. `displayName()` already renders those rows as *Unnamed pharmacy*, which is what makes the row answerable, and this change depends on it.
- **An explicit answer still wins, and the build now says when one is being used.** High Wycombe's config classifies `"pharmacy:"` as `may`, so its sheet keeps that symbol; the generator names that on stderr as `namelessKeptByTier` rather than leaving it to be discovered. One sheet in the estate actually lost a symbol — Huntingdon's internal — and the ink it freed was worth more than the glyph: with the box gone, *Sainsbury's* moved from the end of a leader line to sit directly beside its own symbol.
- **De-duplication stopped deleting the second unnamed place of a kind.** `poi_select.js` compared two blank names, found them equal, and dropped the second unnamed chemist in a town *at any distance* — no candidate, no chooser row, no key and no error. The blank case now belongs to the 60 m proximity test alone, which is the question that arm was always asking. That makes a key collision reachable for the first time, because two survivors are both keyed `pharmacy:`, so `applyTiers` reports `duplicateCandidateKeys` and the generator prints it at build time. Byte-inert across all 18 sheet-drawing maps; no town has two today.
- **Both committed fixtures were re-cut and all three byte gates pass.** `verify:area` reproduces all four St Ives sheets byte-for-byte, `verify:place` reproduces four sheets across two fixtures, and `verify:defaults` still finds all 17 area and 15 place escape hatches changing at least one sheet. The place fixture is High Wycombe Aldi, whose sheets do not move under any of these changes, so it re-cut unchanged — which is the honest answer rather than a missing one.
- **`npm run track:engine` is what carries this to maps already delivered.** Re-vendoring reaches the engine; the packs in the live store go on running the engine they were imported with until they are tracked forward.
