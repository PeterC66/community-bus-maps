title: "R3's checklist sends the approver to the review's own services preview"
---

- **R3 step 3, item 3 (`alternative`), now names the review card's ↗ Open services and stops list link.** That link opens the Service list preview of the version under review. The runbook used to send the approver to the public `/m/<slug>/services`, which shows the version already published and does not exist for a map that is unpublished or unlisted (buses-data OA-296). `docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md` says the same, and adds that the keyboard check can be made on any published map page when this map is not yet public. Docs only; nothing the portal does has changed.
