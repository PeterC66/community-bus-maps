title: "The FAQ answers “a landmark is missing” — and sends the genuinely missing ones to OpenStreetMap"
---

Documentation only, for buses-data OA-261. Prompted by the first sighting of a sheet in the wild: a St Neots councillor’s video at a Love’s Farm bus stop, in which young people had asked for four landmarks by name.

- **`faq.html`, a new entry, `#landmarks`.** *A place people navigate by is missing from our map — how do we add it?* It answers in the order the evidence says the question actually resolves: first the landmark chooser, because the place is usually already on the sheet or waiting under *Show if there is room*; second the category list, because a pub or a café is not drawn whatever its fame; and only third OpenStreetMap, with two links — the [Beginners’ Guide](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Beginners%27_guide) for somebody willing to make an account, and [Notes](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Notes) for somebody who would rather describe the gap without one. It says the addition arrives with the next monthly update as a new place to answer, which is what the landmark-arrivals line shipped on 2026-09-06 exists to show.
- **Why the order matters.** All four places the video named were checked against the published St Neots sheet before this was written: three were already on it and the fourth is a pub. A FAQ that opened with “add it to OpenStreetMap” would have sent every one of those readers to do work that could not change their map.
