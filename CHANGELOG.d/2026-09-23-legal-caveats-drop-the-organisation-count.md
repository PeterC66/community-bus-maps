title: "The terms and legal caveats stop counting organisations"
---

- **`terms.html` and `legal.html` no longer say "one organisation signed up".** Both caveats now read that the service is a pilot and *has no income: no organisation pays for it*, and still link to `faq.html#pilot`, which names the organisations. A count typed into a legal page goes stale the day a second organisation signs up; *no income* goes stale only on the first payment, which is also when the terms review falls due (buses-data OA-138).
- The terms page's *Working draft* caveat and its *last reviewed 25 July 2026* date are unchanged, deliberately: OA-138's park rests on both.
- `docs/PILOT.md` now says where the count lives and names the three pages that still carry it by hand.
