---
date: 2026-09-16
title: "Every named place in Great Britain resolves in the /maps search"
---

- **The *Not ours* panel now answers for any place the Index of Place Names holds, not only for the words the directory itself carries.** Until today a query reached an authority only through one of 76 authority names, the hand-written `covers[]` areas or a town listed as an example, so *Harrogate*, *Lancaster*, *Walthamstow* and every village in England got the honest miss and the letter went with it. A second stage in `src/search/directory.js` now runs when the first finds nothing: the query is resolved through the Office for National Statistics' Index of Place Names (July 2024 edition, Open Government Licence v3.0) to a district, and the district to the directory row for its transport authority. Buses-data OA-312, round 1 of the place-lookup plan of 2026-09-16.
- **What the reader sees.** *Burford is in West Oxfordshire, Oxfordshire, whose local transport authority is Oxfordshire County Council*, then the row's card exactly as before, then one sentence pointing at the map-request route with no pitch in it, then the three other Burfords with their district and county, each a link to the search that lands on that one. A Scottish or Welsh place gets *Kirkwall is in Scotland. This directory covers the 76 English local transport authorities only*. A name in neither the directory nor the Index gets the honest sentence it always got, word for word, plus one line saying the Index was asked too. Every panel that consulted the Index states its edition and licence in a footer line, and `/legal.html` carries the attribution.
- **No guessing, and the order of the two stages is a control.** The authority stage still wins whenever it answers, so *Cambridge* still reads *is part of this authority* and *York* keeps its card; the harness has an arm that swaps the order and requires the control to go red. *Whit* is told it is the start of 427 place names rather than handed a page of them: a prefix hit counts only from four characters and only when eight or fewer places match. A place in the Isles of Scilly, the one English district whose council is not a directory row, is told so rather than given a neighbour.
- **The data is vendored server-side and never shipped to the browser.** `scripts/sync-directory.mjs` now copies `places.json`, `lad-to-lta.json` and `places-source.json` byte for byte from buses-data into `src/search/data/`, and its `--check` in `verify.yml` holds all four files. They are 1.3 MB the panel's server-side rendering reads once at first use; `public/` gains nothing.
- **Seven new harness arms, and one re-anchored.** `scripts/prove-red-directory.mjs` breaks the place stage seven ways — removed, guessing, Scotland resolved as England, the prefix cap gone, the stage order swapped, the honest sentence reworded, the other-places links dropped — and each named check goes red. Arm 4 had to be re-anchored because the authority branch moved two spaces right, and its *changed nothing* guard is what said so.
- **The two checks that used to assert the miss were rewritten, as they said they should be.** *A town the directory does not record is a MISS, not a guess* held *Harrogate* and *Swavesey* and said in its own comment that when it went green by accident the tier fixing it properly had landed. It went red on the first run of this branch, which is that sentence coming true.
