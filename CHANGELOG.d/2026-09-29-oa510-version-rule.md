---
date: 2026-09-29
title: "Accepting an update takes a minor version unless the change is big"
---

- **An accepted update now takes a MAJOR version only when it is a big change.** That means at least half the routes on the map, and at least two, are *transformed*: new, withdrawn, or with at least a quarter of their stops added or removed. Everything else takes the next minor, as a save does. That covers one new or withdrawn route, some stop changes, a new operator or timetable date, reworded destinations, new landmarks and an engine redraw. Until now every accept took a MAJOR, changed or not: of 19 live maps only one had a minor above 0, and Wisbech had reached v12.0. Existing numbers stay as they are (buses-data OA-510, the rule Peter agreed on 2026-09-29).
- **`propose-update.mjs --major` forces a MAJOR** for a redesign the service facts cannot see. The flag travels inside the stored summary as `forceMajor`, so accepting needs no second source and the schema is unchanged. `deliver-map.mjs` forwards it as it forwards `--no-notify`. The staging report, and `--dry-run`, end by saying which number accepting will take and why.
- `diffRouteData()` now records `routeCount`, which counts every route on either side, so a map that gains two routes to four is measured against six. Each `stopsChanged` row also records its stop count `before` the change. A summary stored before this lacks both, so it takes a minor unless forced. The pure `isMajorChange()` and `transformedRoutes()` sit beside it in `src/refresh/index.js`, and `acceptedVersionNumber()` in `src/routes/proposed.js` is the choice the accept route makes.
- `scripts/test-version-rule.mjs` tests both sides of each threshold (a quarter of the stops, half the routes, at least two) and runs the real number choice against a scratch database. Each threshold was broken on purpose (`>=` to `>`, two to one, `--major` ignored, accept always MAJOR, `before` not recorded), and every break turned the suite red.
