title: "The rate-limit sweep runs again, and the container's log has a size cap"
---

- **The five-minute sweep of the public rate-limit counter had not run since 2026-09-03.** The cut that moved the counter into `src/http/helpers.js` exported `rateLimited` and not `sweepHits`, and `server.js` went on calling it inside `try {} catch {}`, so every sweep was a `ReferenceError` nobody saw. `helpers.js` now exports `startHitsSweep(log)`, `server.js` calls it, and neither of the two timers in the listen block swallows its error any more. `npm run test:hits-sweep` asserts that the sweep evicts a closed window and keeps an open one, and that `server.js` imports and calls it with no empty catch; it was seen red against the unfixed code, and red again with the eviction mutated out (buses-data codebase review 2026-09-28, R4 G8, Tier 1.1).
- **`compose.yaml` caps the portal container's log at three 10 MB files.** The json-file driver kept it without limit for the life of the container, and a full VPS disk was the one item of the deferred ops trio that could arrive by itself. The `.dockerignore` and the memory limit were retired as findings on the same day (Peter's Decision C, Tier 1.9).
- **`docs/CONVENTIONS.md` states that `vendor-engine` and `sync-directory` write by default**, as a named exception to `--apply`, rather than flipping them (Decision E, Tier 1.10).
- **Stale totals in comments and on the Background page were replaced with the command that counts them**, not with a newer number: `background.html`'s "Maps for eight towns", `server.js`'s `try` and route counts, `schema.sql`'s pointers to enums that moved to `helpers.js`, and the dependabot, `sync-directory` and CI comments that described mechanisms since removed (Tier 1.8, portal half).
