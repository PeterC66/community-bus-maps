`Development Docs/technical-audit_2026-08-25.md` in `buses-data`, finding N16 (the last P1), and its follow-on N16b.

- **Two rows deleted from `scripts/s6-waivers.json`, five left exactly as they were.** Every one of the seven outstanding S6 correctness passes was run against current data, each with a blind red-team sub-agent on a different model than the orchestrator and a non-bustimes primary source. St Ives (0 hard / 11 soft) and Beaconsfield (0 hard / 22 soft) passed and are cleared. Huntingdon (1), High Wycombe (1), Wisbech (2), March (1) and Ramsey (14) came back BLOCKED; nothing about them was changed, rebuilt or republished. Each run is committed in its town's manifest in `buses-data` with a rendered `verification.docx`.
- **Running S6 made the gate weaker, which is the finding.** `scripts/lib/s6-freshness.mjs` compares dates only — its verdicts are `fresh|stale|never|not-applicable|no-manifest` — and never opens `verification.json` or reads `summary.pass`. Driven through its real interface after these runs it returns `fresh` for Ramsey, which has fourteen hard findings and `pass:false`. The deferral does not cover them either: `gateS6()` consults this file only when the verdict is STALE, so a fresh-but-failing S6 skips the waiver check entirely. Before 2026-08-26 those five towns were stale, deferred and carrying an expiry that would eventually bite; after doing the work the finding asked for, they are silently green. **"Ran S6" and "passed S6" are different claims, and only the first one is gated.**
- **The fix is not applied yet, deliberately.** Making the check read the verdict and fail on `hard > 0` starts red for five live towns, which is the shape this repo already knows gets a gate muted inside a week. It wants dated exceptions in the same spirit as this file, not a bare switch-on. Recorded as N16b rather than done quietly.
- **The file's header now records what the rows cannot.** Two `why` lines misattribute and are corrected in the comment rather than edited in place, since the instruction was to leave failing towns untouched. Ramsey's says "S6 predates the P1-round config pass" — the wording used for St Ives, which passes — when Ramsey is an unattended Tier-2 auto-draft whose S1 was never curated and whose 2026-08-08 S6 already reported nine hard, byte-identical to today's. It was published under a staleness deferral while its own verification said BLOCKED. Wisbech's mentions an X46 palette repick; the red team now says X46 may not exist as a separate service at all, having merged into the plain 46, and the sheet still draws it. Every row's `dataAsAt` also reads 2026-08-19 when the newest S3 everywhere is 2026-08-24, so the rows understate their own staleness by five days.
