title: "A refresh delivery asks the month's ink review, and records its own staging"
---

- **`npm run deliver -- --map …` gains step 0e, which refuses a map in the current ink review unless the review lets THIS build through** (buses-data OA-658, `scripts/lib/ink-review.mjs`). On 2026-10-20 at 01:02 Beaconsfield Simpson Centre v1.46 and Beaconsfield Waitrose v1.40 were delivered by hand, as proposed updates #186 and #187, under accepts for v1.45 and v1.39. Only claude-skills' `stage_refresh.mjs` asked the review, and a hand delivery runs this script directly. Step 0e finds the buses-data tree above `--src`, reads the newest `_gtfs/ink-review_<scan>.json`, and joins the render to its S4 build through the manifest (`basedOn.S4`, else the version). It passes when no ink moved on that build or Peter accepted that build. It refuses a hold, a missing answer, an answer for another build, a review that compared another build, and a build the review already records as staged. A map the current review does not list passes out loud as N/A. A review or manifest that will not parse refuses as CANNOT TELL. `--ink-unchecked "<reason>"` is the one-off escape hatch. It is local, before the scp, and runs on `--dry-run` too. Import mode is unaffected.
- **Step 8 records the staging.** After a healthy restart it runs `ink_review.mjs --staged` from the claude-skills checkout (`SKILLS_DIR`, or a sibling checkout). It passes the proposal number read from propose-update's own success line, `--by` (default `deliver-map`), and `--notify digest` when the delivery went with `--no-notify`. A hand delivery no longer leaves the record to whoever ran it, which is how St Ives Bus Station's #171 sat unrecorded for six days (OA-573). If it cannot record, it prints the exact command and does not change the exit code: the update is staged, and a non-zero exit would read as "try again".
- **Seen red before relied on.** `scripts/test-ink-review.mjs` covers every verdict beside the case that flips it, including the 2026-10-20 case itself. `scripts/prove-red-ink-review.mjs` breaks the gate ten ways, and each arm goes red for its reason.
- **What a customer sees.** Nothing. The script runs on the laptop; no image, sheet or page changes, and nothing needs deploying.
