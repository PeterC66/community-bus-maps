- **Re-vendors `engine/frame_gaps.js` and `engine/place/gen_internal.js` from PeterC66/claude-skills#229 (buses-data OA-519).** These change `design.gapExits`, the opt-in that marks where a route leaves the map between two of its stops. A route that clips the frame for a few millimetres, with no stop in between, now gets one device for the whole excursion rather than a badge and arrow on a sliver too short to see. A route that runs out and comes back through the same point keeps its device when the off-sheet stretch is long. A gap device now carries the route's "to X" when the route has no terminus exit of its own to carry it, and `internalRoads.termini[r].gap` can set that label, or `false` leaves the devices bare.
- **Only Ely Co-op has `gapExits` on.** It was rebuilt as v1.31 on this engine in buses-data, and it is not imported here until Peter has approved its crops (OA-508). Neither gate fixture uses the key, so both are byte-identical and `gate-fixtures/` does not move.
- **Deploy history, written before the deploy per [DEPLOY §3b](docs/DEPLOY.md#3b-the-deploy-history-entry-is-written-before-the-deploy-and-merged-with-it).** This pull request's merge is the commit that goes live. There is no schema or route change and no published sheet changes; after the deploy, `node scripts/track-engine.mjs --apply` brings the stored packs' generators forward.
