title: "The journey planners are named on the FAQ, and deliberately not linked"
---

Copy only, for buses-data OA-260.

- **The *Why a printed map, when there are apps?* answer now names Google Maps, Moovit and Transit**, as the tools that will plan a door-to-door journey you have already decided on, beside the existing pointer to the operator or bustimes.org for times on the day. Naming them is honest about what they are good at, and the answer's own last sentence says what none of them does: show the whole network at once, on a wall, to someone who has not decided anything yet.
- **None of the three is linked, and that is the decision rather than an omission.** Moovit's generated page for the St Neots 61 said on 6 September 2026 that the bus runs once a day at 16:30; the operator's BODS feed and our own record hold seven journeys Mon–Sat. A link is a destination we cannot verify at the moment somebody follows it, and its embed licence is non-commercial only with Moovit free to terminate it. The review behind this is `BusMapsUK/Moovit and the journey-planner apps 2026-09-06.md` in buses-data.
