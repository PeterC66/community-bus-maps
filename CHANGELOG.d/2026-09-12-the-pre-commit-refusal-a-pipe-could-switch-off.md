date: 2026-09-12
title: "The pre-commit refusal a pipe could switch off, and the comment that said it was fixed"
---

- **`git commit … | head -N` could turn this hook's refusal into a landed commit, and the fix believed to have closed that in August never did.** The 2026-08-31 round removed the pipelines from inside `.githooks/pre-commit` and `indent()`'s comment has asserted ever since that a guard can no longer be switched off by `| head`. That reasoning stops a CHILD of the hook being killed and does nothing about the hook's own `echo` taking SIGPIPE on the next line and dying before it reaches `exit 1`. `trap 'exit 1' PIPE` is what actually closes it; the over-claiming comment is corrected rather than deleted, because the loop it describes is still right and just not sufficient.
- **Measured on this repository's own hook, not inherited from the sibling.** In a throwaway worktree of `main`, staging 60 files with trailing whitespace and piping the refusal gave **16 leaks in 24 runs** across `head -2`, `-3`, `-5` and `-8` — the hook printed its refusal, **git exited 0**, and all 60 bad files were committed. The same probe against the fixed hook leaks **0 in 24**, and a passing commit piped at four depths still lands 4 times out of 4, so the fail-safe direction cost no false refusals.
- **It is a race, and the variable is how much the refusal still has to say — not the depth of the cut.** The identical probe with a TWO-line finding leaked **0 times in 32 runs**, because a short refusal fits the pipe buffer and completes before `head` has been scheduled at all. That is the half worth keeping: a clean run of a small case proves nothing here, and it is why the hole survived a round that was looking straight at it. No check in the file was exempt — the stamper block has the same shape as the hygiene one.
- **Found while falsifying an unrelated new guard in `buses-data`,** whose own copy of this hook was corrected the same day (`cbfea924`). Proving a new check can go red is the first thing in months to drive the machinery the old checks share, which is where their holes surface.
- **Hook-only, as it must be.** No CI run anywhere can perform this check: a hook's exit status in a piped terminal is a property of an interactive working tree, which `actions/checkout` cannot express. The required `audit`, `docs`, `test` and `verify` checks are untouched by this change.
