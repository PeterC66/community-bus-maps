date: 2026-08-27
title: "The unchosen tabs now look like buttons, and every tab says what it shows"
---

- **The public map page's tabs were not readable as controls.** Peter's report, with screenshots: "on the Public view it is not obvious that all 3 tabs are buttons". An unchosen tab was a `--muted` word inside a hairline `--border` outline on the page's own white — no fill, no lift, and a hover that changed only the text colour. They are now raised, filled pills in full `--text` with a `--tab-lift` shadow, a hover that turns them the link colour and moves the border towards `--primary`, a `:active` press, and a `:focus-visible` ring they never had. The **chosen** tab moved the other way, from a pale `--primary-soft` wash to solid `--primary`, so which sheet you are looking at is answered by fill rather than by tint. Every state clears AA on `--bg` and on `--surface`, light and dark: `--text` on `--surface` is far above it, hover `--primary` on `--surface-2` is 6.8:1 light and 5.0:1 dark, and the chosen tab is 7.6:1 light and 6.1:1 dark.
- **The editor's tabs had the same fault and it costs more there.** "Even on the Editing view it is not very obvious they are tabs… it is important that they review each output." An unchosen tab was borderless, backgroundless `--muted` text on the group's grey rail. Same treatment: bordered filled buttons on the rail, solid `--primary` when chosen. This reaches the admin console's ten tabs too, which share the `.tab` rules and had the identical problem.
- **Every tab now carries a sentence saying what that sheet shows.** New `OUTPUT_HINTS` in `src/maps/store.js` — one table, not two, even though the LABELS are deliberately two (a reader and an editor are told different things about the same sheet, but what is *drawn* on it does not change with who is looking). `outputHint(key, kind)` returns the place wording where a place map's sheet is genuinely a different picture and falls back to the area wording otherwise, so the two sheets that differ carry two sentences and the three that do not carry one. It reaches the editor through `outputsForClient()` and the public page through `publicOutputs()`.
- **The sentence is not only a tooltip, because a tooltip reaches neither a keyboard nor a phone.** Both pages get a `.tab-hint` line between the tab row and the artwork — a new `#sheetHint` in `public/map.html`, a new `#tabHint` in `views/app/editor.html`, refreshed on every tab change. The first attempt put the public page's copy into the existing `#sheetNote`, which sits BELOW the viewer; a screenshot of the real page is what caught it, and a description that arrives after the thing it describes is not a description. `title` carries the same text on hover in both.
- **Verified as a picture, not as a passing build.** Headless Chrome against a seeded portal on an isolated `DATA_DIR`: the live `/m/march` page with each tab chosen in turn, and a harness page carrying the four real tab rows (editor, editor-hovered, admin, public) against the served stylesheets, in light and in dark with the dark tokens spliced verbatim out of the served `styles.css`.
- **Tabs also gained the `aria-selected` they were missing.** All three rows declare `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`, and only the public page was setting the state — the editor's preview tabs and its compare dialog announced nothing.
- Portal bumped `0.9.4-pilot` → `0.10.0-pilot`; `docs/C1-customer-user-guide.md` follows its "Applies to" line.
