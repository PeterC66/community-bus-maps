- **Re-vendors `engine/area/gen_external_radial.js` from claude-skills #300 (buses-data OA-437, A1 slice 2).** `engine/vendored.json` is restamped.
- **What changed in the engine.** When the legend on a map's "Buses from" sheet has no clear ground and the town set no `legendWrap`, the engine now wraps the longest operator row narrower itself, widest wrap first, instead of leaving the legend on top of the diagram. A wrap a town typed still wins, and `design.legendFit: false` keeps the old behaviour. A wrapped legend's panel now counts its badge grid, not only the operator name after the last row.
- **No gate fixture moves.** The sheet is identical, old engine against new, on all 12 stored town references, including the five that set `legendWrap`.
- **What a customer sees.** Nothing yet. A map picks this up the next time it is rebuilt without a hand-set `legendWrap`.
