date: 2026-09-17
title: "Seven public pages said we have no customers, and we have had one since the Love's Farm maps went live"
---

- **`faq.html`, `apply.html`, `background.html`, `accessibility.html`, `legal.html`, `terms.html` and `examples.html` all asserted, in one form of words or another, that no organisation has signed up.** `GET /api/public/maps` says otherwise and has done for some time: **Love's Farm Community Association**, `isDemo: false`, publishes **four** of the eighteen maps here under its own name — `st-neots`, `st-neots-town-centre`, `st-neots-tesco-extra` and `st-neots-co-op`. The remaining fourteen are ours, under `BusMaps.uk (pilot)`. Every sentence now says one organisation, four maps, no income.

- **The pilot framing itself is untouched, deliberately.** *Pilot* is a claim about the SERVICE, and the site-wide banner and `[Pilot]` tab prefix are meant to survive the first organisation publishing — that is what buses-data OA-320 settled on 2026-09-12, and it is why the red *SAMPLE MAP* band is a separate, per-map question. One organisation paying nothing does not make this a finished commercial product, and nothing here pretends otherwise.

- **"No income" is kept everywhere it appeared, because it is still true.** They pay nothing. `background.html` is the page where that mattered most: its sentence ran *"no customers and no income; every map here is one we made ourselves"*, which was wrong twice over, and the two halves are now separated rather than deleted.

- **`examples.html` was the seventh page and was found only by looking.** Six were found by grepping for the claim; that one says the showcase maps are ours and that *"no organisation publishes them"* — and the showcase includes `st-neots-town-centre`, which is one of the four. A sweep for the sentence would never have reached it, because the falsehood is in the join between the sentence and the list of maps beside it. The sheet stays in the showcase and the sentence gains the exception, rather than the example being dropped to make a sentence true.

- **What prompted it.** A letter to the owner of bustimes.org went out on 2026-09-17 saying *"One organisation has registered: a community association in St Neots, which now publishes four of those maps under its own name"* (buses-data CORR-009 message 004). If he had followed the link, the site would have contradicted the letter on seven pages.

- **Not changed, and flagged rather than decided: `faq.html` still opens that section with *"it has never been used in anger."*** That is now arguable — one organisation publishes four maps, and a councillor printed one of our sheets for a bus shelter — but it is also the sentence carrying the honest weight of the whole pilot framing, and "one organisation and a bus shelter" is not use in anger either. The corrected bullet beneath it now does the factual work. Left for Peter.
