- **Re-vendors `engine/area/gen_external_radial.js` and `engine/place/gen_external_places.js`, and adds `engine/legend_key.js`, from the claude-skills branch `oa305-hybrid-legend` (buses-data OA-305, Peter's choice 2026-10-01).** The *Operators & services* legend now badges a route only when a spoke on that sheet draws it, or when the map declares it in `routes.json` `localLoops[]`, which keeps the badge and adds a grey caption row such as *local circular*. An operator with nothing left to badge loses its row. Before this, 20 badges on 8 of the 17 external sheets in the estate had no line under them. The first half of this change landed in #453 with the logic inline in both generators. This entry's pull request moves that logic into the shared `legend_key.js` and gives it a row in `engine/vendored.json`.
- **No gate fixture moves.** Neither fixture has a spokeless route, and `npm run verify:area` and `npm run verify:place` reproduce every fixture sheet byte for byte.
- **What a customer sees.** Nothing changes on deploy, because no stored sheet is re-rendered. After `npm run track:engine`, the next render of a map that has a spokeless route draws the shorter legend. That is Wisbech, March, Ramsey, St Ives, St Neots and High Wycombe, plus the St Neots Tesco Extra and St Neots Town Centre place maps. Those towns gain their `localLoops` captions when they are rebuilt in buses-data.
- **Deploy history, written before the deploy per [DEPLOY §3b](docs/DEPLOY.md#3b-the-deploy-history-entry-is-written-before-the-deploy-and-merged-with-it).** This pull request's merge is the commit that goes live.
