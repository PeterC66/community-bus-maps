date: 2026-08-28
title: "The first genuine public report, and the runbooks that assumed it could not happen"
---

- **A member of the public in Ramsey read the published external map and reported three faults. All three were real.** The X31 showed **Whittlesey**, which it does not serve — it runs via **Pondersbridge**; and **Bury** was missing from both the X31 and the 32, which serve it on a one-way loop out of the town, along with **Wistow** and **Upwood**. Checking the report against the map's own stop chains found a fourth nobody had mentioned: the 303 runs **Chatteris–Ramsey–Huntingdon** and Chatteris had no spoke at all. The map was **unlisted the same day** and is verified gone — `/m/ramsey` and `/m/ramsey/services` both 404, absent from `/maps` and `sitemap.xml`. The cause was in the map-building engine, not the portal, and is fixed in `claude-skills` (`625447b`) with a falsification harness; the remaining work on the map itself is `buses-data` OA-153. The incident is recorded in ops P3.
- **`R6`'s pilot paragraph asserted two things that had quietly stopped being true.** It said *"a member of the public acted on a wrong map is currently a hypothetical"* — as of 2026-08-28 it is not — and it described the site as sitting behind a `robots.txt` `Disallow: /`, which has not been so since indexing was split from pilot mode on 2026-08-21. A wrong sheet is now reachable from a search engine and not only from someone we told. Both are corrected in place rather than deleted, because each was load-bearing for the severities below it.
- **The `SAMPLE MAP` band is on the rendered sheet only, and the text alternative does not carry it.** `src/render/pilotStamp.js` overlays every rendered output, so the sheet a reader downloads is marked. The `/services` page — which exists precisely so that a screen-reader user or anyone who prefers text gets the same information — presents the same service data with the pilot banner but no sample marking on the content itself. Recorded in `R6` rather than fixed here: the accessible alternative getting the weaker warning is a decision worth taking deliberately, not a typo.
- **`R6`'s "tell the publishing organisation" step now says what to do while every organisation is ours.** There is no third party to hand it to, and the temptation in that situation is to treat the fix as the whole response and skip the record. The incident row, not the commit, is what tells a later session why a map is unlisted.
- **`R5`'s message-queue step now says to check a report against the data, and to check the same field on every other row while you are there.** One reported defect on one route became four once all 253 stops on all eight routes were resolved. A reporter can only see what is wrongly *present*; what is wrongly *absent* has no witness but the person it turned away — and on that map the omissions were both the majority and the more harmful half.
- **`R6`'s link to the incident log was dead** — it pointed at `incident-log.md`, and the file is `P3-incident-log.md`. It sat on the exact path somebody follows in the middle of an incident.
