title: "The place external gets its how-to panel, and three geometry faults go with it"
---

- **The vendored place external generator moves for the first time since it was imported, carrying OA-019's round three whole.** Four changes, one file (`engine/place/gen_external_places.js`), and every one of them measured across all NINE shipped place externals rather than the six the backlog's own arithmetic named — that measurement globbed `Areas/*/Places/` and never looked in `Places/_standalone/`, where Ely Co-op and both Godmanchester Co-ops live. Walk for fixtures; do not glob for them.
- **`hubEdge()` was dead code and the ellipse in it had not run for months (OA-149).** It ended `Math.max(16, 1/denom)`, and `1/denom`'s own maximum is `HUB_A` = `HUB_W/2 + 3` with `HUB_W` floored at 26 — so the floor and the computation's ceiling were the same number and every spoke on every sheet started at exactly 16.00mm regardless of bearing. Two sheets had ONE distinct start radius across every spoke they draw. The floor is deleted rather than re-tuned, because the ellipse already carries the 3mm pad the floor was standing in for; start radii now span 9.5–16.0mm with 4–14 distinct values per sheet. This is also why OA-060's badge ring came out exactly circular: two independent floors had cancelled a computation between them, and the symptom surfaced somewhere else entirely.
- **A dashed spoke painted a hairline at its end, and the obvious fix made it worse (OA-160).** Fitting the pattern to a whole number of cycles was tried first and took the estate from 2 slivers to SIX — ending on a cycle boundary is the most fragile place to end, because coordinates round to 2dp and a hair past a boundary is a hair *into* the next dash; one spoke came out at a 0.0046mm tail, an order of magnitude finer than the defect. Aiming for the middle of a gap instead leaves 1.15mm of clearance either side, 250× the rounding error. Of 14 dashed spokes, 7 ended inside a dash and 2 of those under 0.1mm; none does now.
- **The Operators panel was sized by counting characters while real Arial advances sat required in the same file (OA-061).** `font_metrics.js` was already powering `hubFit` and `badgeFit`; the legend never got it. Switching the four `panelMaxX` sites narrows the panel by 3.6–22.5mm on nine of nine, and every panel HEIGHT is unchanged — the note wrap's 100mm floor absorbs the narrowing exactly, which is the half of that row that explicitly warned against being changed in the same breath.
- **`design.howToUse` now exists on the place external, on by default, and the bullet count is the finding (OA-056).** A six-bullet panel could not be placed on six of the nine sheets. Narrowing the column is the wrong lever and the sweep says so out loud: 92/84/76/68/60/52mm place on 3/2/1/3/5/6 sheets — **not monotonic**, because a narrower column wraps into more lines and it is a page device's HEIGHT that collides on a sheet whose spokes fan out horizontally. The lever is the bullet count, and **three bullets place on 9 of 9**. That is the same answer all eight TOWN externals reached independently by pinning their own bullets to three, which had been read as eight separate local decisions and is one general fact about this sheet family.
- **Two conditional bullets were removed rather than defaulted around.** `minutesToDestination` is present on all nine sheets and a tick label is drawn on all nine, so the two `if`s were constants wearing a condition — and each of them cost six sheets their panel. A branch no fixture can take the other way is a dark branch whether or not it is written as an `if`.
- **The portal's own place fixture was recut in `buses-data` and pushed BEFORE this branch existed.** `verify.yml` checks that repo out with no `ref:`, so a PR's byte gate runs the PR's engine against whatever is on its `main` at that moment; opening this first would have produced a truthful red about a 38,595 B reference measured against an engine that writes 39,505 B. Three delivered map packs were brought forward with `npm run track:engine`, so the fix reaches maps already in the portal rather than only new imports.
