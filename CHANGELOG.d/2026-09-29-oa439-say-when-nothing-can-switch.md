date: 2026-09-29
title: "The Landmarks panel says so when no kind of place can be switched"
---

- **When not one category on a map can be switched, the line under the boxes now says so** — *None of these can be switched on this map yet: its data holds none of them. Ask us if you want them.* — instead of promising that switching "takes effect when you save" over a row of boxes that will not tick. Tried on Beaconsfield on 2026-09-28, whose portal payload predates the categories, the old line read as a broken page. The usual sentence returns on any map where at least one box can be used (buses-data OA-439 item 5a).
- **A disabled category box now looks disabled**: it is drawn faded with a not-allowed cursor, and so is its label, where before only the label's colour changed.
