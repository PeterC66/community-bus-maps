date: 2026-08-24
title: "The opt-ins became defaults, and every fixture was recut on the result"
---

- **Three vendored files moved and the frozen fixtures were rebuilt in the same change.** `engine/place/gen_internal.js`, `engine/place/gen_external_places.js` and `engine/expert/gen_boarding.js` carried the 23–24 August review round plus three newly promoted defaults. This is the drift that `changing-the-engine.md` §4 warned would not be byte-inert, and it was not: `verify:place` was watched go **red** on the vendoring alone — the internal missed by 41 bytes and the boarding sheet by 115 — before `Places/_portal-fixture/High Wycombe Aldi` and `High Wycombe High Street` were recut from the skill's new v1.14 / v1.4 builds. Both now reproduce byte-identically, and `FIXTURE_DIR` moved to St Ives v6.55 / Huntingdon v3.42 / Beaconsfield v1.46.
- **`design.sheetQr`, `design.keyCols` and `design.howToUse` are defaults, not opt-in keys.** All 20 maps set `sheetQr:{mm:14}`; 17 of the 18 that draw a Key set `keyCols:2`; all 8 towns opted into `howToUse`. Each keeps an escape hatch, and each was proved byte-inert before the keys were stripped from the configs — every sheet built from a stripped config is identical to the sheet built from the explicit one.
- **All three joined `verify:defaults`, and two of them are not `false`-shaped.** The escape-hatch suite now covers 16 keys on the town side and 13 on the place side. `keyCols` is a NUMBER, so its hatch is the old value `1` — `keyCols:false` reads as 0 and falls back through `|| 2` to the default, which would have made the row assert nothing while looking green. `sheetQr` only draws where `design.sheetUrl` is set, so it is testable here and would not be on a fixture cut from a map with no address. `howToUse` is deliberately absent from the PLACE list: the panel lives in `gen_external_radial.js`, which no place generator is, so there is nothing on a place sheet for it to change.
- **An empty stand caption is no caption.** With `anchorTick` off by default (23 August), a stand whose bearing NaPTAN does not give produced an empty `<text>` element on the boarding sheet — three of High Wycombe Town Centre's five. Invisible on paper and not invisible everywhere: the quality metric counts `<text>` nodes. Found by the rebase, which is the first thing to re-render every boarding sheet since.
- **`npm test` and all four verify suites green.**
