title: "The last three blocks out of the internal generator"
---

- **Re-vendored `place/gen_internal.js` and took three new modules with it** — `complexity_ladder.js`, `north_arrow.js` and `feature_labels.js`, extractions 10, 11 and 12 of OA-129 Phase 3. All three are required at LOAD by the generator, so the portal had to receive them in the same commit or throw `MODULE_NOT_FOUND` on the first internal render anywhere. `engine/vendored.json` gains a row each, written by hand: `--update` restamps rows that exist and will not invent one.
- **The require scan named all three before the rows were written, which is the second time it has been watched do its job.** Copying the generator across on its own gave `UNRESOLVED  required through SKILL_ASSETS by place/gen_internal.js` for `complexity_ladder.js`, `feature_labels.js` and `north_arrow.js` — the state that, a day before the scan was fixed, reported "every module they require is here". Doing it in that order deliberately is worth the thirty seconds.
- **Byte-inert, proven on the portal's own fixtures.** `npm run verify:place` reproduces all four skill-rendered sheets across both fixtures byte-for-byte, and `npm run test:selfsufficient` now resolves **17** modules out of `engine/` and one from the map data pack, with none from anywhere else. On the skills side every one of the 74 sheet verdicts is identical to the pre-extraction baseline, and so are the SVG hash, `data-kind` count, exit status and normalised stderr of all 20 maps under `EDITOR_KEYS=1`.
- **What each module is for.** `complexity_ladder.js` holds the four rungs that make a big town readable — `internalCorridors`, `coreBox`, `stopThinning`, `corridorPalette` — with the "absent ⇒ identity" property that keeps the other seventeen towns byte-identical. `north_arrow.js` holds the compass in the three pieces it has to stay in: the angle decides the footprint and must be known early, the position cannot be settled until the ink is stamped, and the drawing happens last. `feature_labels.js` sites the NAME of a river, road, railway or canal, and holds the four guards that refuse a name nobody can read — the one that caught Wisbech's "River Nene" struck across the Services list is in there.
