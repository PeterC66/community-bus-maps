title: "The badge and lozenge separation reaches the portal"
---

- **Six vendored engine files move forward at once, and four of them carry behaviour.** `svg_primitives.js` gains a badge **de-duplication** in `badgeStack` and a whole `separateRow` solver; `gen_internal.js`, `gen_external_radial.js` and `gen_external_places.js` call into them from `drawTermBadges` and `townNode`. That is OA-023/OA-024's badge placers and OA-060/OA-147's badge-on-badge and terminus-lozenge separation — board-wide, badge overprints went 70 → 30 → 0 and lozenge overlaps to 0, the latter measured for the first time because until then no tool could count them. `schematize_internal.js` and `gen_internal_place.js` also move but differ by **0 non-comment lines**; they are the tail of a comment-only change.
- **The portal really was behind on behaviour, and the row that explained the drift away was the reason nobody noticed.** OA-145 was opened for three files whose only difference was comments and it told its next reader, in as many words, not to read DRIFTED as "the portal is behind". That was accurate when written and stopped being accurate the moment a different change touched one of the same files — and a DRIFTED row looks identical either way. Twelve maps had been rebuilt in `buses-data` onto code this portal did not have. The byte gates could not see it: they gate that repository's maps against that repository's engine, and both moved together.
- **The fixture had to be recut first, and was proven to discriminate rather than recut into agreement.** `Places/_portal-fixture/High Wycombe Aldi` was a `build 1.15 · 26 Aug 2026` snapshot, so `verify:place` went red on `external.svg` and `internal-schematic.svg` the moment this engine landed — both DIFFERING at an unchanged byte length, which is what a geometry nudge looks like. It was recut from the map's own v1.16 render in `buses-data` (pushed first, per OA-018) and then falsified: with the recut fixture in place and this engine reverted, those two sheets are RED and the other two green. `verify:place` is now PASS on 4 sheets across 2 fixtures, and `npm run verify` and `npm test` are green throughout.
- **This is the re-vendor half only.** Re-vendoring reaches the portal; it does not reach the maps already delivered into it, which carry their own generator copy — `npm run track:engine` is what does that (OA-130), and a republish is what puts a changed sheet in front of a reader. Both are still outstanding under OA-123.
