title: "A route badge is no longer dropped for brushing a landmark's corner"
---

- **Re-vendored `engine/place/gen_internal.js` and `engine/label_placer.js` from the claude-skills pull request for buses-data OA-470.** A landmark symbol is round, but the space kept for it was a square. After the OA-250 change, Beaconsfield's twin pharmacy symbol moved half a millimetre. Its square then clipped one corner of a 380 route badge's space, and the badge was dropped without any warning. A street name, "Maxwell Road", was then printed in the gap, and the pharmacy symbol covered its first letter. The route-badge pass now treats each landmark symbol as the circle it is drawn as, so a badge that only touches the square's corner is placed. Across all 23 maps with a street sheet, only Beaconsfield's moves: the badge is back and the street name no longer prints under the symbol. Both portal fixtures (St Ives and High Wycombe Aldi) are byte-identical, so `verify:area` and `verify:place` do not move.
- **Deploy history, written before the deploy per [DEPLOY §3b](docs/DEPLOY.md#3b-the-deploy-history-entry-is-written-before-the-deploy-and-merged-with-it).** This pull request's merge is the commit that goes live. It carries only the re-vendor and this entry. No schema, route or UI changes. The published Beaconsfield sheet redraws at its next re-render. §3a has no sheet-level check that can see this. The claude-skills estate measurement in the engine commit is the evidence.
