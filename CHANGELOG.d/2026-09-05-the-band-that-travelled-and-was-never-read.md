title: "The band that travelled with every area map and was never read"

buses-data OA-088, the portal half of the town-complexity plan. The bus skill scores every town at the end of S2 — GREEN, AMBER or RED, with the measure that tripped it — and RED is the one verdict in the whole pipeline that says *do not build the standard single sheet; choose a strategy first*. S4 pulls S2's outputs into its run folder, S5 copies them on, and `import-map.mjs` keeps every `*.json` in a payload, so `complexity.json` has ridden with every AREA delivery since the first one. On 2026-09-05 all eight area maps on the live host held theirs, **one of them RED and published**, and the string "complexity" appeared in this repository only inside the vendored engine. The verdict was computed, was right, travelled, and was read by nothing — the exact shape of `build-warnings.txt` five days earlier (OA-046), for the other file the engine writes about its own work.

- **The review screen now says the band, in three registers.** `readComplexity()` in `src/maps/store.js` sits beside `readBuildWarnings()` and follows its one rule: absent, unreadable, unrecognised, or a band the gate never writes all report `null`, never a guess. The review route returns it; `public/app/review.js` renders RED in the same warning register as a blocking build, AMBER and GREEN as a hint line, and names the remedy keys the build carried and when the score was taken. A pack scored before 31 August 2026 is said to be a four-measure score, because the points-of-interest measure did not exist then — which is why High Wycombe's live pack reads GREEN while the laptop's reads RED.
- **Two silences, deliberately different.** A PLACE pack carries no score, because the gate scores towns, so null on a place says nothing. Null on an AREA is a gap and is said in as many words: *the town's complexity score did not travel with this version*. "We do not know" must not look like "fine", and the test asserts both silences by name.
- **The decision records what the approver was told.** Approval writes `complexity: { band, failed, scoredAt }` into the publish evidence beside the checklist, and the decided view shows it. A scoreless pack writes no key at all rather than a null one, so a later reader cannot mistake "not recorded" for a band.
- **The map-requests screen tells the admin to score an area before approving it.** Approval is the quota gate, and a RED town is not a single-sheet job. The scoring itself is the laptop's — bus-work now prints the town's band beside an area request, or says it is unscored and how to score it without spending quota; Peter's own guide had promised that since 21 August and the tool never did it.
- **`scripts/test-complexity-band.mjs`** asserts the reader on invented shapes and on the newest S2 run of every real town when the map tree is present, the route, the evidence, both silences, and that the importers still keep every `*.json` — the one line that makes the band travel at all. Proven red twice before it landed: an absent file guessed as GREEN fails five assertions, and an approval that drops the band fails three.
