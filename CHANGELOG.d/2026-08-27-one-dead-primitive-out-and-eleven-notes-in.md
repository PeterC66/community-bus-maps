title: "One dead primitive out, and eleven notes about what nothing draws"
---

- **`cross(x, y, col)` is gone from `engine/svg_primitives.js`** — the one genuine retire out of OA-136's dark-feature register. It had no caller anywhere: not in a generator, not in the portal. `place/gen_external_places.js` has a `cross()` of its own and it is the eight-argument segment-intersection test, a different function sharing the name, which is why it read as used for as long as it did. Removing it moved no bytes on any of the 20 maps — which is what dark means when it is true.
- **Eleven engine paths now say, at their own site, that no committed sheet takes them**, each marked `DARK, measured 2026-08-27` so the set is greppable: the CLASSIC (`internalRoads:false`) drawing model and five more projection branches, the whole v1 label placer, the railway tie symbol and `minSegLen`, the entire hand-adjust path for a linear feature, the `panelCols` layout, the `panelScale` opt-out, `subFit`'s print-floor refusal, `coreBox`'s hand overrides, the whole object form of `stopThinning`, and the `STRICT_GUARDS` refusal path. **None of them is dead code** — each is a live feature a config key selects — and none is certified by any byte gate, which is exactly why the note belongs beside the code rather than in a backlog row.
- **One claim in that register did not survive re-measurement, and the correction is in the code.** It said only `panelCols.keyAt` had ever been read on High Wycombe. Instrumenting says that town's two 49 mm columns come from `panelCols.cols` and `panelCols.width`, **read by the `panelCorridors` branch**, which sets neither of its own. Three of the four keys are live; only `panelCols.row` goes unread. What is dark is the layout, not the config.
- **Byte-inert, proven both sides.** `npm run verify:place` reproduces all four skill-rendered sheets across both fixtures byte-for-byte; `npm run test:selfsufficient` resolves 17 modules out of `engine/` and one from the map data pack, none from anywhere else; `check-vendored` accounts for all 28 engine files. Skills side, all 74 sheet verdicts are identical to the pre-Phase-3 baseline, and so are the SVG hash, `data-kind` count, exit status and normalised stderr of every one of the 20 maps under `EDITOR_KEYS=1`.
