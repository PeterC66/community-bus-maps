title: "Every map's footer claimed the same cross-check month, and it was wrong on nineteen of them"
---

- **"cross-checked at bustimes.org (June 2026)" was a literal in three generators, so all 20 maps said it and only March was telling the truth.** The service research each sheet was describing actually happened between June and August 2026 — and on Ramsey it had never happened at all when its sheets first claimed it had. This is the same footer a member of the public read before reporting four real faults on that map; a provenance line is what tells a reader how old the timetable behind a sheet is, so it is not a cosmetic string. The date now comes from each map's own `checkedAt`, set from its own S1/P1 date, and the vendored engine is re-vendored to match (`claude-skills` `84ae114`, `buses-data` `19fc96b`).
- **Where a map does not say, the sheet now says nothing rather than guessing.** An absent `checkedAt` omits the parenthetical entirely. It is deliberately not defaulted from `validFrom`, which answers a different question — when the timetable takes effect, not when anyone last checked it against the operator — and the two already disagree on Huntingdon, whose data is valid from June but whose research was done on 12 July. Both dates now appear on that sheet and they correctly differ.
- **A byte gate could never have caught this.** It compares a generator against its own previous output, so a date that is wrong on every map reproduces perfectly and for ever. The new guard reads the generators as source and refuses any month-and-year literal in them; its first version was itself worthless — it resolved the engine path itself, so all three of its mutations survived `prove-red` because the harness had pointed `ENGINE_DIR` at a scratch copy the test never read. 173 mutations, 173 caught, once it went through `_engine.js`.
- **`scripts/refresh-place-fixture.mjs` is new**, because the High Wycombe Aldi fixture had to be recut and there was no supported way to do it. It reuses the verifier's own scratch-and-vendor path, so the reference it writes is by construction what `verify:place` will regenerate. Worth knowing about that recut: the SVGs are the **same size** before and after, since "June" and "July" are both four letters — a four-character content change no size check would show, which is why the gate compares bytes.
