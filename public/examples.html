Examples

A fixed showcase of what the system produces. These are maps we made ourselves to test and demonstrate it — no organisation publishes them, and they are not maintained as live travel information. Everything that has been through the portal end to end is listed separately.

Genuine output of the system — nothing here is mocked up. Each shows an internal map (left) and an external map (right). Click any image to see it full size.

Area St Ives

A whole-town map: every service within St Ives, plus where those buses go.

Outputs shown: internal (geographic) · external

Place St Neots Town Centre

Centred on one point: the buses that serve the town centre and where they can take you.

Outputs shown: internal (geographic) · external

Place High Wycombe — Aldi

A supermarket on a busy main-road corridor: eleven services call here and fourteen places are reachable from the door. A place map copes with that by naming where you can get to rather than drawing every route.

Outputs shown: internal (geographic) · external

Each map can also be produced as a straightened schematic and a tube-map-style diagram. Colours, labels and which outputs to include are all adjustable.
